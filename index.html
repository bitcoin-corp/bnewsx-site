How it works

Readers pay writers. Nobody in between.

Live

Headlines as threads

Stories from CoinGeek, Hacker News, Ars Technica, BBC Tech and WIRED open as threads in the bWalletX Feed. Discuss on chain; swipe a story away when you're done.

Live

Write it, keep every version

Bitcoin Writer encrypts your draft in the browser and saves each version on chain. You pay only for the bytes you save.

Coming

Pay to read

Writers set a price per story. Readers pay a few cents, and the money goes straight to the writer. Free stays free.
